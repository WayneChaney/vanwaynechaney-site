# Footage Prompts For The Kinetic Promo

The animated system maps are already built in Remotion. The footage needed is Wayne, not generic office B-roll.

Record horizontal 16:9 video, eye level, chest-up, in a clean real workspace. Put Wayne on the opposite side from the text when possible. Soft natural or key light, solid dark shirt or jacket, no readable screens in frame.

Save the four selected takes in `remotion/public/media/` with these exact names.

## `wayne-leadgen.mp4`

Say: "Lead generation. Capture, qualify, book. When someone raises their hand, the system puts the next move in front of your team."

Performance: direct and fast. Leave one second of silence at the front and back. Do not point at the camera.

## `wayne-followup.mp4`

Say: "Follow-up happens before attention goes cold. No chasing texts. No guessing who owns the next response."

Performance: calm, confident. Use one small hand gesture on "before attention goes cold."

## `wayne-operations.mp4`

Say: "Operations should be less manual and easier to see. Clear handoffs. Real visibility. Work that keeps moving."

Performance: steady, conversational. Keep your hands below chest height.

## `wayne-close.mp4`

Say: "I am Vanwayne Chaney Jr., a computer engineer from Miami University. Find the bottleneck. Move faster."

Performance: hold eye contact on the final line, then pause for two seconds.

## Optional Music

Add a licensed, percussion-led instrumental as `music.mp3`. It should be energetic and clean, with no vocals and no cinematic swell. The edit is designed to work without it.
