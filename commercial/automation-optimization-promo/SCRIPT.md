# Vanwayne Kinetic Promo

**Format:** 30 seconds, 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps.
**Reference:** The direct editorial confidence of Solthane, rebuilt for Vanwayne's automation work. It is not a copy of Solthane's branding or page.

## The Creative System

This is a kinetic systems commercial, not a generic slideshow. Orange is the action signal. Warm white is the operational workspace. Dark ink is the pressure and credibility layer.

The visual story is specific:

1. A business loses momentum in the gaps.
2. Lead Generation becomes a moving three-step pipeline.
3. Follow-Up becomes a route from trigger to owner to response.
4. Operations becomes an intake-to-handoff-to-visibility map.
5. Wayne closes on camera as the engineer who builds the system around the bottleneck.

## Voiceover

Revenue leaks in the gaps.

A lead waits. A handoff breaks. A task disappears. Small delays cost real money.

I build practical systems that keep work moving.

Lead generation. Capture, qualify, book.

Follow-up before attention goes cold.

Operations that are less manual and easier to see.

I am Vanwayne Chaney Jr., a computer engineer from Miami University.

Find the bottleneck. Move faster.

## Production Rule

Use real client outcomes and reviews only after the exact result and permission to identify or anonymize it are verified. This 30-second cut makes no unverified revenue claim.
