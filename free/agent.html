4 jobs an AI agent can take today

For owners who keep hearing "AI agent" and want to know what one actually does.

An AI agent is software that does a task start to finish on its own, like answering a text or sending a reminder, and checks with you when it gets stuck.

The 4 jobs

  1. Answering missed calls with a text and getting the details.
  2. Following up on quotes that went quiet.
  3. Chasing late invoices with friendly reminders.
  4. Booking appointments and sending the reminders.
