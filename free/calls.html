The missed-call text setup

For owners who can't answer every call because they're on a job.

The 3 pieces

  1. A business number that rings your cell first. You answer like normal when you can.
  2. If you miss it, the caller gets a text about 25 seconds later. Something like: "Sorry I missed you, this is [name] with [business]. What can I help you with?"
  3. Their reply comes to your phone like any other text. You answer between jobs.

Do this today

Open your phone's call log and count last week's missed calls from numbers you don't know. That's how many people the text would have caught.

Example

MADE-UP SHOPA two-person heating and cooling shop misses about 15 calls a week in July. With the text in place, the callers who write back get a time on the schedule instead of calling the next company on Google.

The rules

  • Texting customers from a business number in the US means registering it with the carriers. It's called A2P 10DLC.
  • Only text people who called you, and stop the moment someone replies STOP.

If it isn't working

  • Texts not going through? The number's registration probably isn't approved yet.
  • People think it's spam? Put your business name in the first line.
