How ChatGPT picks who to recommend

For owners who want to know if AI is sending customers to them or to the shop down the street.

When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best roofer near me," it doesn't just read your website. It looks at what other sites say about you.

Where it tends to look

  1. Your Google Business Profile and the reviews on it.
  2. Review sites like Yelp, and the directories for your trade.
  3. "Best of" lists and local news that mention you.
  4. Your own website. It counts, but it's only one piece.

Do this today

Ask ChatGPT the question your customers ask, like "best [your trade] in [your town]." Write down who it names and which sites it points to. Those sites are where you need to show up.

Example

MADE-UP SHOPA salon owner asks ChatGPT for the best salon in her town. It names two competitors and points to a local "best of" list and Yelp. She isn't on the list and has 4 Yelp reviews, so she knows exactly where to start.

If it isn't working

  • Different answer every time? Ask 5 times and count who comes up most.
  • Not showing up at all? Make sure your business name, address and phone number read the same on every site.
