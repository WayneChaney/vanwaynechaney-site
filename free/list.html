3 public records that show who has to sell

For real estate investors and agents who want to reach sellers before the house hits the market.

The 3 records

  1. Behind on property taxes. County treasurers publish delinquent lists.
  2. Open code violations. Cities track these, and a lot of them post them online.
  3. Probate. When an owner passes away, the house often has to sell to settle the estate.

All three are public record.

Do this today

Go to your county treasurer's site, find the delinquent tax list, and pull 20 addresses in the zip you buy in.

Example

MADE-UP INVESTORAn investor pulls one zip's tax list, checks it against open code cases, and ends up with 12 houses on both lists. Those are owners under real pressure, and nobody else is calling them yet.

If it isn't working

  • List too big? Stack two records: behind on taxes AND an open code case.
  • No phone numbers on the list? That takes a skip trace, which is a separate step.
  • Before you call anyone, check the Do Not Call list.
