The Google Maps checklist

For local business owners who want to show up when someone nearby searches for what they do.

Google says it ranks local results on three things: relevance, distance and prominence. You can't move your shop, so this list works on the other two.

The checklist

  1. Fill out every part of your Business Profile. Main category, extra categories, services, hours, service area. Google can only match you to searches for things you told it you do.
  2. Ask every happy customer for a review, and answer every review you get. Good or bad. Send the link by text right after the job.
  3. Add new photos every week. Finished jobs, your team, your truck, your storefront.
  4. Post a short update every week. A finished job, a seasonal tip, what's new.

Do this today

Open your Business Profile and check your main category. If it doesn't match what people actually search for ("Plumber," not "Contractor"), change it. It takes two minutes, and local search surveys rank it the top factor you control.

Example

MADE-UP SHOPA two-truck plumbing shop has 9 reviews, no new photos in a year, and "Contractor" as its category. They switch the category to Plumber, text a review link to their last 20 customers, and post one job photo a week. About 20 minutes a week.

If it isn't working

  • You show up for your business name but not for "plumber near me"? Recheck the main category and the services list.
  • Reviews stalled? Ask the same day as the job, not a week later.
  • Profile stuck in verification or suspended? Never make a second listing. Google pulls both.
